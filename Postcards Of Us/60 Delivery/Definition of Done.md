# Definition of Done

A product or engineering change is done when:

- Acceptance criteria are explicit and pass.
- Success and failure states are verified.
- Ownership and privacy impact is checked.
- Mobile behavior is considered when the change touches the core workflow.
- The relevant test, smoke check, or operational evidence is recorded.
- Linked roadmap, backlog, risk, and ADR notes remain accurate.
- User-facing copy and source documentation are updated when needed.
