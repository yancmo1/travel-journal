# Success Metrics

## Beta health

- Invited households activated
- Households completing at least one journey
- Memories added per active household
- Return visits to existing memories
- Private shares created and opened
- Support requests by category
- Failed login and upload rate

## Quality gates

- Zero confirmed cross-household data leaks
- Zero confirmed photo privacy bypasses
- Backup freshness within the documented target
- Restore drill completed successfully
- Core mobile workflow passes on both primary phones

## Learning questions

- Which feature causes a family member to return?
- Which step creates the most onboarding friction?
- What do families ask for after seeing a complete journey?
- What would they pay for, and what would they expect that payment to include?
