# Beta Scope

## In scope

- Invite-only household access
- Username/password sign-in with the existing JWT session flow
- Add and edit travel memories
- Attach, organize, and view photos
- Group stops into journeys
- Maps, routes, search, timeline, and On This Day views
- Traveler tracking and cleanup tools
- Private journey sharing
- Export/import safety net
- Personal onboarding and support

## Explicitly deferred

- Social login during beta
- Video uploads
- Unrestricted public signup
- Public social-network features
- Billing before value and operating costs are understood

## Beta exit conditions

- Core workflows are reliable on primary phones.
- Household boundaries are tested server-side.
- Photo privacy is verified.
- Backup restore has been proven.
- Beta evidence supports the next product and hosting decisions.
