# Product Principles

1. **Private by default.** Family memories should not become public by accident.
2. **Story over database.** Data entry exists to create a meaningful family story.
3. **Completed journeys beat feature count.** A small finished story is better than an abandoned collection of tools.
4. **Warm, calm, and trustworthy.** The product should feel like a keepsake, not an administration console.
5. **Evidence before expansion.** Beta behavior and conversations decide what comes next.
6. **Safe recovery matters.** Photos and memories need exports, backups, and understandable account recovery.
