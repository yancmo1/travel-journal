## Current hypothesis

Families will value a private, low-friction way to turn scattered travel
records into a complete story they can revisit together. The core loop is more
important than a broad feature catalog.

## Questions that must be answered

- [ ] Do invited families return to revisit memories without prompting?
- [ ] Which part creates the strongest repeat value: maps, journeys, photos, or keepsakes?
- [ ] Does private sharing lead to additional family participation?
- [ ] What support burden remains after the concierge beta?
- [ ] Which feature is worth paying to preserve or improve?
