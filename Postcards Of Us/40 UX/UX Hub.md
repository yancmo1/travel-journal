## Experience target

Warm, calm, legible, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a family
keepsake with useful structure, not a spreadsheet or public social feed.

## Design constraints

- Privacy and sharing state must be clear at the point of action.
- The mobile core loop must work with comfortable touch targets.
- Empty, loading, offline, and failure states need useful next actions.
- Date uncertainty should be represented honestly rather than forced into false precision.
- Photos should lead the emotional experience while the data remains searchable.
