# Onboarding Flow

1. Receive an invitation or operator-created account.
2. Sign in with the current username/password flow.
3. See a clear explanation of what is private and who can access the household.
4. Add one meaningful memory with a photo.
5. Group it into a journey when enough memories exist.
6. Share one selected journey privately.

## Later

Social login may reduce friction after beta, but it must fit the invitation,
household membership, recovery, and duplicate-account rules.
