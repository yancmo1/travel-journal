# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Invite-only beta

**Status:** Current

- Keep the existing authentication flow.
- Invite a small number of families.
- Validate the core workflow: add a place, attach photos, group memories into a journey, and share privately.
- Monitor storage, backups, failed logins, uploads, and support issues.
- Fix beta blockers before adding broad account options.

## Phase 2 — Paid founding beta

**Entry signal:** Families complete journeys and report repeat value.

- Harden onboarding and account lifecycle.
- Confirm pricing and hosting costs.
- Improve tenant isolation and photo privacy evidence.
- Consider social login for lower-friction account creation and sign-in.

## Phase 3 — Public product

- Enable carefully controlled public signup.
- Add supported OAuth providers and account-linking flows.
- Offer reliable account recovery, exports, deletion, and session revocation.
- Move infrastructure toward managed or independently hosted production services as usage justifies it.

## Explicitly deferred

- Social login during the beta.
- Video uploads in the first beta.
- Broad public signup before tenant isolation and privacy checks are complete.
- Billing before product value and operating costs are understood.
