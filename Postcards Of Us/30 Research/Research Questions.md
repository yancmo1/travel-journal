# Research Questions

## Product

- What makes a family member return to an existing memory?
- Is the journey view more valuable than the individual place record?
- Do maps, timelines, photos, or keepsakes create the strongest emotional response?
- Does private sharing bring another family member into the product?

## Onboarding

- Which step is hardest for a new household?
- Is manual help acceptable beyond the first beta?
- What import path would save the most time?

## Account access

- Do testers ask for Google, Facebook, or Apple sign-in?
- Is password recovery sufficient for the beta audience?
- Would social login create confusion around household invitations or duplicate accounts?

## Commercial

- Which capability feels worth paying for?
- What hosting, storage, and backup promise would feel trustworthy?
