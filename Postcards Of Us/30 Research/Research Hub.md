## Evidence standard

Tag findings as:

- **Observed** — behavior directly seen in the app
- **Reported** — participant or family statement
- **Documented** — primary documentation or operational record
- **Inferred** — reasoned conclusion that still needs validation

Record contradictions. Do not convert one enthusiastic request into a product
commitment without checking repeat behavior or asking why it matters.
