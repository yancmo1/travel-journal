# Interview Plan

## Goal

Understand whether families return to preserve and revisit memories, what they
find difficult, and which improvements would justify a hosted product.

## Conversation prompts

- Tell me about the last time you looked back at a family trip.
- Where are the photos, dates, and notes today?
- What would make this story feel complete?
- What would make you trust a private family archive?
- What would make sign-in or inviting another family member easier?

## Capture rule

Record evidence and language, not conclusions. Put conclusions in a decision or
feature brief only after comparing multiple conversations.
