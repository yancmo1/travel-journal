# Travel Timeline

_Last updated: 2026-07-31_

This is a working travel-history timeline assembled from the Carnival cruise ledger supplied by Yancy Shepherd and a read-only search of Gmail.

## Scope and confidence

- The Carnival cruise table below is the authoritative cruise ledger, including sail dates, ships, destinations, booking numbers, and VIFP-point changes.
- Gmail was searched with an All Mail-style query using `in:anywhere`, which reaches archived mail and can also include Spam or Trash. The broad scan covered approximately 2,177 matching messages from January 2010 through July 2026. Promotional mail, price alerts, duplicate reminders, and marketing messages were excluded where possible.
- `Confirmed` means a booking or itinerary document was found. `Completed` means there is additional evidence such as a boarding pass, post-stay message, or post-trip parking record. `Planned` means the travel is still in the future as of the last update. `Needs grouping` means the record is real but its relationship to a larger trip is not yet certain.
- No email was modified, archived, labeled, deleted, forwarded, or sent during this research.
